Main points

IdeaGenerating isn't free

A seed doesn't "contain" the picture in readable form. You pay with computing time to unfold it. Deeper zoom → more iterations → more time.

ProvenMost things can NOT come from a small seed

There are 2ⁿ possible results of n bits, but fewer than 2ⁿ⁻ᶜ seeds shorter than n−c bits. So at most 1 in 2ᶜ results can be squeezed by c bits (c = 20 → at most 1 in a million).

Small rule → huge structure works only for a rare, special fraction. Real question: why does our world seem to be in that fraction?

⚙ Advanced
DefinitionKolmogorov complexity

K(x) = length of the shortest program that prints x. "Small seed, big structure" means K(x) ≪ size of x.

ProvenK(x) is not computable: you can find a short seed, never prove in general it's the shortest.

OpenNo shortcuts? (irreducibility)

Bennett's "logical depth": short seed, long run. Wolfram: for some rules nothing is faster than running them. For Rule 30 this is a conjecture.

ProvenRandomness vs information

Dice lab: random rolls, fixed shape → randomness is not information. Cell rule with random start: the random cells are information and change the result.

OpenPhysics: short laws, but what about the start?

Physics = laws (short) + initial conditions (maybe huge). For the whole universe to be "a small seed", the starting state must be simple too. Whether it is (low-entropy Big Bang, "Past Hypothesis") is open.
