Experiment

1 · Geometry
The symmetry of the obstacle is not the symmetry of the experiment: the initial field, the outer edge and the square grid all take part. After Start the lab reports which symmetries of the grid the whole state really has.
2 · Initial wave field
Contours follow
A standing field starts at rest and splits into an inward and an outward part. A travelling field starts already moving. They are different initial conditions, not two views of one.
3 · Physics
Obstacle wall
Outer edge
Domain
∂²u/∂t² + γ ∂u/∂t = c² ∇²u. A scalar wave equation: it can look like water, but it is not a fluid simulation.
4 · Release behaviour
Release moment
Rule: a released cell starts at rest with zero displacement. With a fixed obstacle this adds no energy; with a free obstacle the step it creates is measured and shown.
5 · Visualization
All views read the same simulation state. A contour is a line of equal displacement, not a moving material surface.
