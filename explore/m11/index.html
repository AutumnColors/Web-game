M11 symmetry

11 points, 66 blocks of 5, 7920 symmetries.

Tap any 4 dots.
Blocks preserved: 66 / 66

The rule. Every 4 points lie in exactly one block of 5. Tap 4 dots and the 5th lights up.

The symmetry. After picking 4 dots, tap any 4 spots as targets. Exactly one of the 7920 symmetries sends your dots there, and every block still lands on a block.

The contrast. A random shuffle of 11 points almost always breaks most of the 66 blocks. Only 7920 out of about 40 million shuffles keep them all. Those 7920 form the Mathieu group M11.
