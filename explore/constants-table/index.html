The table of physical constants

The five constants

Each one is a switch for a whole area of physics: speed, quanta, gravity, heat and charge. Together they are the constants Planck built his natural units from. Turn a switch off by taking a limit, and that area of physics disappears.

Patterns in the 5 × 5 table

Observation

ħ has repaired three broken cells. Heat + light gave the ultraviolet catastrophe, charge + light made atoms collapse, and classical heat capacities failed at low temperature. Each was fixed by adding ħ, a constant outside the pair. Intuition, not proof: a broken cell may point to a missing constant.

Observation

Every open cell contains G. G–ħ (quantum gravity) and G–ke (the hierarchy) are the unsolved pairs. G is also the least precisely measured constant here (about 2 parts in 100,000), while c, h and kB are now exact by definition.

Observation

Direction matters only in some pairs. c–ħ and c–G have two historical routes that meet at the same theory. G–ħ has two routes that have not met. c–kB asks two different questions. The rest give the same physics either way.

Groups

Gravity and charge share one group. Both are 1/r² forces with the same hidden SO(4). Group theory can't see the 10³⁶ difference between them; only the number does. And the only cells with ? are the G–ħ cells.

Caveats
  • "Start with X, add Y" here means a historical or logical route, not a defined mathematical operation. This is not yet a group table.
  • kB and ke are arguably unit conversions. The unit-free strength of charge is α = kee²/(ħc) ≈ 1/137, which already mixes three switches.
  • Switching a constant off really means taking a limit (c → ∞, ħ → 0, G → 0). Order-of-limits questions are the precise version of "direction".

Patterns in layer 3

Groups
Unknown physics = unknown symmetry. Every cell marked ? is an open cell, and every one involves G. Where physics is solved, a symmetry group is known.
Groups
SO(4) keeps appearing. Any 1/r² force has a hidden SO(4) symmetry, so gravity and charge share it: Atoms, Hierarchy, Giant atom. The group can't tell gravity from charge; only the 10³⁶ strength ratio does. Relativity breaks SO(4) down to SO(3) (Fine structure).
Groups
The only finite group in the table is S_N, in quantum statistics (Condensation, White dwarf, Halt). A5 lives inside S5, so this is the one place where your A5 world touches the physics directly. That's intuition, not a result.
Observation
Every open cell still contains G. The pattern from the 2D table survives into 3D: Quantum GR?, Planck, Unification?, Heating? and Weakest all involve gravity.
Observation
ħ keeps repairing. Blackbody (heat + light + ħ), Lamb shift (Maxwell + ħ) and Halt (clumping + ħ) each fix a broken or incomplete pair. The one broken cell left, Catastrophe (Maxwell + heat), is fixed only one layer up, when ħ is added as a fourth constant.
Observation
The last step decides the content. Most of the time, the order of the first two steps changes the history but not the physics. Sibling cells that share the last constant usually share the word.
Correction
In chat I listed ħ–G–kB and ħ–G–ke as empty triples. They aren't: white dwarfs (Fowler, 1926) live in the first, and the "giant gravitational atom" thought experiment and atom gravimetry live in the second.
Proof vs intuition
Proven: the 60 ordered triples match the 60 elements of A5 one to one (A5 is sharply 3-transitive on 5 objects), once (c, ħ, G) is chosen as the identity. Not proven: that the A5 class of a cell means anything physically. The "A5 class" coloring is there so you can look for a pattern, not because one is known.
