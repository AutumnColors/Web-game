Area and volume are cell-center approximations. Includes only cells connected to the Dead Sea seed at the selected level. Volume includes an approximate modeled underwater floor and is not calibrated to published volume. DEM water pixels represent the existing surface.

Drag to rotate, pinch or scroll to zoom. The terrain comes from AWS Terrarium elevation tiles, sampled on a regular geographic grid. Flood volume uses a connected-cell grid calculation; water visualization is still an approximate mask.

3D underwater floor is an approximate distance-to-shore model, not measured bathymetry; surveyed contours remain a separate overlay. DEM does not measure the underwater lakebed. Coastal detail and narrow channels are not reliable at this preview resolution.
