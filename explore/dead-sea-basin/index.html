Area and volume are cell-center approximations. Includes only cells connected to the Dead Sea seed at the selected level. Volume is additional water above −440 m, not total lake volume. DEM water pixels represent the existing surface.

Drag to rotate, pinch or scroll to zoom. The terrain comes from AWS Terrarium elevation tiles, sampled on a regular geographic grid. Water is a visual elevation mask, not yet a hydrologic flood-fill. No volume or shoreline area is calculated in this version.

Bathymetric contours are a separate surveyed map overlay, not yet used in volume integration. DEM does not measure the underwater lakebed. Coastal detail and narrow channels are not reliable at this preview resolution.
