For real c, the boundedness set is rotationally symmetric about the real axis; the 3D volume is sampled from its 2D radial profile. For nonreal quaternion c, the complete quaternion recurrence is evaluated at every 3D sample. The 3D slice fixes initial k = 0. Projection mode samples initial k in [-2.15, 2.15] and colors the fraction of sampled values that do not escape within the iteration cutoff. Escape radius 4, finite iteration cutoff. Rendering uses interpolated marching tetrahedra with continuous exterior potential and gradient normals. The surface is a finite-resolution approximation; very fine fractal features remain unresolved.
