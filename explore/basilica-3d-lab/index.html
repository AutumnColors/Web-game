Mathematical definition: At each real x, compute r(x) = max{y ≥ 0 : x + iy is numerically bounded under z² + c}. The displayed surface is (x, r(x)cos θ, r(x)sin θ). This rotation is a chosen 3D construction, not a unique 3D Julia set. The blue and purple animated bands are a visualization applied to the reconstructed surface, not physical rotation of Julia-set components. The outer envelope omits internal gaps. For nonreal c, rotation is only an illustrative envelope because the Julia set is not generally symmetric across the real axis. Period-two proximity color measures the first sampled near-return after two iterations. It is not necessarily attraction, especially outside the period-two stability region.
