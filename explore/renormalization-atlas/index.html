Scope and normalization

These are examples of real analytic unimodal period-doubling classes, not an exhaustive list of universality classes. Critical order is the first nonzero power near the critical point. The functions shown are computed from 128-step return maps at estimated accumulation parameters, with g(0)=1 and signed α. Parameter estimates and function fits use finite precision. The fixed-point equation characterizes the limiting function, not the finite polynomial displayed.
