What is being calculated?

At the estimated accumulation parameter, q is the critical point and sₖ = f²ᵏ(q) − q. The plotted function is gₖ(x) = [f²ᵏ(q + sₖx) − q]/sₖ. Here the exponent means iteration, not a power. Every curve passes through (0,1) by construction; agreement elsewhere is measured, not imposed. Level 7 is a numerical reference, not exact g*.
