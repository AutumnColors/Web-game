Stroke N is exactly N steps along the grid. Every step one edge from an earlier line scores +1.
Match the board's max and the stroke scores ×2. Pass through an apple for ×2; match the best apple path too for ×4.
Landing on a line, a bomb, or your own path ends the scored game.
