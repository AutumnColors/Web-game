Mine­sweeper

The classic minefield, rebuilt from scratch. Level 1 plays by the original rules. New levels will bend them.

How it plays
First dig
Mines are laid only after your first click, so it is always safe and always opens a patch.
Numbers
Each open tile shows how many mines touch it, across all eight neighbours.
Zeros
A tile with no mines around it opens its neighbours by itself, and the clearing cascades.
Flags
Right-click, long-press, or switch to Flag mode to mark a mine.
Chord
Click a number whose mines are all flagged to open every other tile around it.
