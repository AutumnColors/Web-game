Numerical inverse Böttcher map ψ(w), with w=exp(G+iθ), reconstructed by inverse iteration from large |w|. Root signs are chosen by external angle continuation. The approximation becomes less reliable very close to G=0; no interior points are represented. Colors are normalized per image. Use the Cartesian lab to compare the original geometry. This is a coordinate transformation, not a claim that Euclidean distances are preserved.
