GEOMETRY

The obstacle is an exact circle. The initial wave contours are concentric rings generated directly from radial distance. No Mandelbrot orbits are used in this control experiment.

SIMULATED PHYSICS

After release, a numerical 2D wave equation evolves the displacement. The circle interior starts at rest. The outer field is held fixed as a reservoir of incoming wave structure. This is a wave model, not a fluid or water-flow simulation.

Compare with the Mandelbrot lab using the same density and speed. The circle is a symmetry control, not an equal-area comparison unless you calibrate the original obstacle area.
